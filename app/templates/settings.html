How holidays work

Each category uses its own holiday list (Categories tab). Without one: the additional category's list, then the default calendar, then greytHR's own holiday marks. An employee's page can override.

Optional holidays are working days unless greytHR marks the person's day as a holiday (they took it).

Lists change every year: upload next year's list (or paste it) — a full year replaces that year's dates; dates that moved are removed. A Day column is checked against the date.

Targeted holidays (below) apply only to the categories, locations, groups or people you choose.

Weekly offs follow the category's pattern (Categories tab) unless set on the employee's page. Work from home on a person's own weekly off or holiday is not counted as WFH — it is off-day work.

Bulk load: greytHR's Holiday List Importer files (Holiday Date, Description, Is Optional, Location, Category) or the template (Date, Holiday, Category / Calendar, Is Optional, Day; For Categories / Locations / Groups / Employees → targeted).
