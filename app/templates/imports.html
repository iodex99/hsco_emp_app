Upload files

Select one or more Excel files. Each sheet is recognised automatically from its headers; extra sheets with manual working are ignored. Filters and hidden rows in the files do not matter — every row is read. Re-importing a month replaces that month's data.

What to upload each month

  1. Attendance Muster Report (greytHR) — required. Day-wise status, in/out and hours.
  2. Employee Swipe Details — punches, WFH / client / office locations, coordinates if present.
  3. Date of Joining Report — joiners and the active-employee list (exits are suggested).
  4. Attendance Summary Report — optional, kept for reconciliation.

One-time / occasional: Employee master (groups, weekly-off pattern, exemptions), Holiday list, Leave opening balances, or a previous manual MIS sheet (used to seed groups and to reconcile).
